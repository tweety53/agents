# plan-agent-pair.sh gains a <role> argument — intended rewording
**Every planning dispatch** takes its pair from `plan-agent-pair.sh <seed> <n>` — `<seed>` the change name (in `/flow-plan` before capture, the session token; for the self-review and in-run fix planners, their own `Key:`), `<n>` counting the session's planning dispatches from 1 — passing the printed model as the Agent tool's `model` and the effort as its `effort`.
Each dispatch's model and effort come from `plan-agent-pair.sh <your Key> <n>`, `<n>` counting your planning dispatches from 1, and every dispatch prompt carries the line `Role: explorer` (an Explore agent) or `Role: plan-designer` (a Plan agent) and the two sentences of **Propagate this** (`~/.claude/rules/agent-baseline.md`) verbatim.
The planner runs section **B** of `skills/flow/brainstorm-planner.md` without <!-- refs-guard:allow --> plan mode, reading the ticket and every file under `<abs-worktree>/.superpowers/sdd/planner/`, dispatching its own Explore and Plan agents (it records their rows, `-role explorer|planner`, `-key explore-<n>|design-<n>`, each pair from `plan-agent-pair.sh <name> <n>`, `<n>` continuing the run's count across rounds — the parent names the next free `<n>` in each round's prompt, since a reused key restarts the earlier round's row), publishing or updating the prototype a visible change needs, and writes `round-<r>.md`: the design draft, the settled prose the convergence confirm shows, then `## Questions` — each question with two to four options, one marked recommended, the convergence confirm last when nothing else is open.
The session may dispatch the fresh Explore and Plan agents **Explore and design agents** (`skills/flow/brainstorm-planner.md`) allows — its pair from `plan-agent-pair.sh <session-token> <n>`, seeded with this session's token — never a `fork`; the session itself does the thinking: it reads the tree, runs the investigate-then-ask rounds and the convergence check below, and creates the change or offers the `design.md` capture directly.
**Every planning dispatch** takes its pair from `plan-agent-pair.sh <seed> <n> <role>` — `<seed>` the change name (in `/flow-plan` before capture, the session token; for the self-review and in-run fix planners, their own `Key:`), `<n>` counting the session's planning dispatches from 1, `<role>` the dispatch's own `Role:` line, `explorer` or `plan-designer`, whose row the hook admits it on — passing the printed model as the Agent tool's `model` and the effort as its `effort`.
Each dispatch's model and effort come from `plan-agent-pair.sh <your Key> <n> <role>`, `<n>` counting your planning dispatches from 1 and `<role>` that dispatch's own Role, and every dispatch prompt carries the line `Role: explorer` (an Explore agent) or `Role: plan-designer` (a Plan agent) and the two sentences of **Propagate this** (`~/.claude/rules/agent-baseline.md`) verbatim.
