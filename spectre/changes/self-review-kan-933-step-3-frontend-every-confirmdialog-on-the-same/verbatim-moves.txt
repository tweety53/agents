# Self-review fix: a ui-path change runs the whole visual verify suite in the parent's full-suite step, so a moved capture fails before flow.verify (kan-933's 14 verify failures).
Before `verify`, `check-dev-stack-fresh.sh <canonical-worktree>` exit 1 restarts the stack by that section's start rule, and a failed restart is reported and stops the step; exit 0 or 2 proceeds.
When the change's diff touches a `## visual verification` `ui paths` glob (`git -C <worktree> diff --name-only <merge-base>..HEAD | check-visual-trigger.sh <worktree>` exits 0 for any worktree in the resolved set, its merge base from this run's working notes; exit 1 or 2 for every worktree skips this run) and `decision.json` does not record `visual.verify` `skipped`, the list ends with that section's `verify` run whole — `<specs>` empty, every capture — against the canonical worktree's stack, under the stack rules of **One stack for the verification tasks** below, so a capture a shared-component change moved fails here rather than at `flow.verify`.
A failure a product defect caused is fixed in this step's fix round; a capture the change moved on purpose — one whose difference the diff makes as design.md or the task's `**Files:**` intend — is re-recorded in it: its committed PNGs are deleted and `capture` runs with `<spec>` substituted for its spec, writing them as PNGs that do not yet exist, and they are committed by the regression checkout's rules, step 12 of `skills/flow/visual-verify.md`.
`verify` runs from the resolved regression checkout (the project root when none is declared), after the section's `setup` row when the toolchain is missing.
