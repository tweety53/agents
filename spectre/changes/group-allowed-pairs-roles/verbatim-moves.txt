Each `###` heading below is one pipeline area, and a role sits under exactly one; a role's unmarked row carries its `Does` cell, a plain-English line on what the role does, and a marked row's `Does` is `—`.
flowd serves both on `GET /api/v1/roles`, and the Experiments view groups its roles table by them.
