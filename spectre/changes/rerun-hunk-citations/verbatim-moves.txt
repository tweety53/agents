# kan-964 self-review: a fix-round re-review's verdict lines cite the fix-diff hunk they read,
# checked by check-rerun-citations.sh — new rule text, added on purpose.
**The parent checks every diff-reading re-run's verdict lines before reading its findings**, as that slot's `flow record dispatch end` is recorded, with `<report>` its `panel-report-<round>-<id>.md`, `<diff>` the diff its dispatch named, and one `F<n>` per finding it was sent to re-review:
A `fixed` verdict whose citation lands in no hunk of that diff is rejected mechanically and the re-review sent back.
Given a diff, your report file carries one verdict line per finding you were sent to re-review, in exactly this shape: `verdict: F<n> — fixed — <path>:<line>` or `verdict: F<n> — not fixed — <path>:<line>`, where `<path>:<line>` is a line inside the hunk of that diff you read for that finding (`none` in its place, on a `not fixed` line, when the diff touched nothing of it).
check-rerun-citations.sh <report> <diff> F<n> [F<n>…]
| panel bundle, at most two per round | `reviewer` | `panel-<round>-<slot\|slot+slot+slot>` | `skills/flow/review-panel.md`, **Bundled dispatch** |
A second exit 1 counts that re-run as not clean, and each finding still `UNCITED` goes to the handback below.
Exit 1 sends the re-run dispatch back **once**, on the same model and effort — one send-back per failing re-run dispatch, not one per role: its key is the failed re-run's own key plus `-cite`, `-key panel-<round>-<slot|slot+slot+slot>-cite`, and its prompt carries every `UNCITED:` line of every role in that dispatch; it sits outside the panel bundle's two-per-round cap, at most one per re-run dispatch.
