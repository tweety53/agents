# Operator, 2026-10-02: each status carries its own leading emoji. Every sentence below was added or reworded on purpose for that.
A status line is `<emoji> <unit> (<a few words on what it is>) — <state>`, one per unit, and nothing else, with one distinct emoji per state: ✅ done, 🔄 in progress, 🔍 in review, ⏳ pending, ⛔ blocked: `✅ Task 22 (spec text) — done`, `🔄 Task 21 (finished-workout look) — in progress`.
Not how the unit got there (closed, ticked, pushed), not why it is in that state, not what comes next.
