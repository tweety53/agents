A failing `flow.visual-verify` also has its stage mark closed beside that row, `flow stage end -command '/flow' -stage flow.visual-verify -outcome stopped <name>`, and a run that re-enters this loop while `flow.write-in-progress` is open closes that mark the same way first, so the round's next `flow stage begin` supersedes no open run (kan-1016: `flow.visual-verify` attempts 1–9 and 11 closed `superseded`).
A recorded decision is a named cause only for the deltas it names, by element and property: any other delta on the same band or element is unexplained until measured (kan-1016: a band's `colour` delta was put down to decision `warmup-sublines-w4b` for four rounds, and was the checkbox's size).
Each composed frame carries its own `- <frame id> matrix:` line, never one line for a range of frames: `check-verify-report.sh` refuses a `done` step 10 while a frame with a `- <frame id>: <composite path> diff=` line has none (kan-1016: one line covered nine frames, and the warm-up sheet's frame had no matrix until round 8).
Its output is saved once, to `<changeRoot>/visual-verification/matrix/<frame id>.json`, overwritten every round — never a second copy under another name (kan-1016: each frame's matrix was committed three times under three names).
The composite's panels are the capture left, the frame middle and the difference right, unlabelled; a radius, a stroke width, a glyph size or a colour is read from `<stem>.frame.png` and the capture PNG themselves, never off the composite (kan-1016: a stepper radius read off the composite was raised in two rounds and withdrawn).
The list also carries every frame the `<spec>.mockups` sidecar of each capture spec this change edits maps, whether or not its baseline has moved yet, and two frame ids are never read as one (kan-1016: the warm-up sheet's frame `templates-A4b`, mapped by the edited `templates.spec.ts`'s sidecar, joined the list only in round 7, once its baseline moved).
