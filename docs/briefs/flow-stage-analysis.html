  • A stage left open is closed after 6 hours with outcome abandoned; those runs and open runs are out of the duration percentiles and counted on their own.
  • Dollar percentiles use costed runs only; the uncosted count stands beside them.
  • No column marks a fix run.
  • suite_runs carries no change or stage link, so suite time cannot be tied to a stage.
The query 
SELECT s.stage,
       count(*)                                   AS runs,
       count(DISTINCT s.change_id)                AS changes,
       percentile_cont(0.5) WITHIN GROUP (ORDER BY (s.metrics->>'cost_usd')::numeric)
         FILTER (WHERE s.metrics ? 'cost_usd')    AS median_usd,
       percentile_cont(0.9) WITHIN GROUP (ORDER BY (s.metrics->>'cost_usd')::numeric)
         FILTER (WHERE s.metrics ? 'cost_usd')    AS p90_usd,
       sum((s.metrics->>'cost_usd')::numeric)     AS total_usd,
       percentile_cont(0.5) WITHIN GROUP (ORDER BY extract(epoch FROM s.ended_at - s.started_at))
         FILTER (WHERE s.ended_at IS NOT NULL AND s.outcome <> 'abandoned') AS p50_s,
       percentile_cont(0.9) WITHIN GROUP (ORDER BY extract(epoch FROM s.ended_at - s.started_at))
         FILTER (WHERE s.ended_at IS NOT NULL AND s.outcome <> 'abandoned') AS p90_s,
       round(count(*)::numeric / count(DISTINCT s.change_id), 2) AS attempts_per_change,
       max(s.attempt)                             AS highest_attempt,
       count(*) FILTER (WHERE NOT s.metrics ? 'cost_usd') AS uncosted,
       count(*) FILTER (WHERE s.outcome = 'abandoned')    AS abandoned,
       count(*) FILTER (WHERE s.ended_at IS NULL)         AS open
FROM   stage_runs s
WHERE  s.command = '/flow'
  AND  s.harness = 'claude-code'
  AND  s.started_at >  timestamptz '2026-11-13T13:34:13Z' - interval '30 days'
  AND  s.started_at <= timestamptz '2026-11-13T13:34:13Z'
GROUP  BY s.stage;
